#   English term = 中文        the term is translated the way you write it; the first time it appears
#                              the English follows in brackets: 多孔板（multi-well plate）
#   English term = -           hard to translate: just the English in brackets: （galvanometric mirror）
#   English term = 中文 !      always show the English in brackets after the Chinese (otherwise only the first time;
#                              --brackets always does that for every term)
#   ~ heard = right            Whisper keeps hearing "heard": the English itself is corrected
